4-Player Mahjong Style Analyzer
(MajSoul Stats / 雀魂牌譜屋連動)

How to use

  1. Import from MajSoul Stats (amae-koromo) with a player name, account ID, or player-page URL — or fill the 12 fields manually. Jade room stats are recommended (the model was fit on Soul-level Jade data).
  2. Coverage is limited to games amae-koromo has recorded (public ranked Gold+); many accounts will be missing.
  3. Click Analyze to see your intensity, playstyle type, and plot position.

Original Japanese tool: 四麻スタイル分析 by 悠楽うらめ. Method write-up: note.com explanation.
